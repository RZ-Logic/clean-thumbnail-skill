# Post demos: sources and license

These three posts, the brands **Stillroom** and **Cedar Café**, the workshop, dates, prices and offers are fictional gallery examples. They do not advertise actual events or services, and the stock model is a neutral stand-in, with no implied endorsement.

- **Creator workshop:** Ron Lach, [A Man Creating Video Content](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-man-creating-video-content-8360444/). The existing `creator-photo.jpg` is 3840×2560; [its source credit](creator-photo-LICENSE.md) records the original. The post is rendered from the original `creator-photo.jpg` with `--backdrop solid` (no input preparation); the supplied face box takes fractions of that original photograph.
- **Studio offer:** Curtis Adams, [Indoor view of office room with desk and laptop at home](https://www.pexels.com/photo/indoor-view-of-office-room-with-desk-and-laptop-at-home-11125425/). - **Café event:** Chevanon Photography, [Close-up of Coffee Cup on Table](https://www.pexels.com/photo/close-up-of-coffee-cup-on-table-312418/), Pexels photo 312418. [Original download](https://images.pexels.com/photos/312418/pexels-photo-312418.jpeg?cs=srgb&dl=pexels-chevanon-312418.jpg&fm=jpg). The 4016×6016 original was resized to 2400×3595 with Pillow Lanczos and saved as `post-coffee.jpg`. The renderer then applies `--crop 0,0,1,0.9` and the top type band.

No scene or face was generated or repainted. The workshop and studio posts are rendered directly from the original photographs, so no prepared input files exist; their type is added by the normal post renderer, and no input contains baked message text.

## Demonstration logo

`post-stillroom-logo.png` is an original transparent demo asset, 1262×240, with a simple room-plan mark to the left of the lowercase word **stillroom**. It is a fictional identity, not an official company logo. It was drawn with Pillow; the wordmark uses bundled Playfair Display SemiBold. The drawn mark and composition are supplied under the repository's MIT license; the font uses the [SIL Open Font License](../skill/clean-thumbnail/fonts/playfair/OFL.txt). The stock photos are not part of this mark.

## Rendered files

The `post-creator_4x5`, `post-coffee_4x5` and `post-studio_4x5` masters, 1080 copies and previews are generated by `skill/clean-thumbnail/scripts/thumb.py --mode post`, using the commands in the README's [Feed Posts section](../README.md#post). `post-gallery.jpg` is an overview sheet assembled from the 1080 copies, with labels added using the bundled fonts.

`post-demo-report.json` records the exact supplied body and details, the wrapped lines and sizes returned by the renderer, contrast, the creator's face visibility and warning results. All body and details words were checked against the supplied text in order. Every 1080 copy was inspected, and every dark/light 360 px feed preview was visually reviewed.
